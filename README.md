# SentixAI

### AI U.S. Stock Intelligence Agent powered by Bitget Reality

SentixAI connects directly to Bitget Reality U.S. Stock market data and turns live market information into clear, explainable intelligence.

## What SentixAI Does

- Live U.S. stock prices from Bitget Reality
- Historical market data and multi-period trends
- Technical analysis including RSI, momentum and SMA20
- Fundamental analysis and valuation indicators
- Market catalyst detection
- AI-generated market thesis
- BUY / HOLD / SELL signals with confidence scores
- Opportunity ranking across stocks
- Stock-to-stock comparison
- Natural-language AI market chat
- Search across Bitget Reality U.S. stocks
- No API key required for public market data

## Supported Intelligence

SentixAI combines:

**Market Data → Technical Analysis → Fundamentals → Catalysts → AI Reasoning → Signal**

The goal is not simply to display a price. SentixAI explains *why* a stock is receiving its current signal.

## Bitget Integration

Market data is sourced directly from Bitget Reality U.S. Stock APIs.

Example Reality symbols:

- `rAAPLUSDT`
- `rNVDAUSDT`
- `rTSLAUSDT`
- `rMSFTUSDT`
- `rAMZNUSDT`
